\begin{abstract}
We study the fastest transport exponent $\alpha_u^+$ of supercritical analytic quasi-periodic Schr\"odinger operators at the worst phase. For finite Liouville exponent $\beta$, an upper law involving the Lyapunov exponent $\ell$ and acceleration $\omega$ keeps transport below one uniformly in phase. When $\beta\ge\ell$ its local rate is $1-\ell/(2\omega\beta)$, and the factor $2\omega$ is attained. For the almost Mathieu operator with $L=\log\lambda>0$, we prove the sharper uniform upper bound $1-L/(2\beta)$ also on $L/2<\beta<L$. Thus whenever the even-denominator exponent satisfies $\beta_e=\beta>L/2$, with $\beta$ finite, the uniform exponent, the supremum over phases and the exponent at phase $1/4$ all equal $1-L/(2\beta)$. The same value holds on a dense $G_\delta$ set, while almost every phase has $\max\{0,1-L/\beta\}$. The new upper proof uses translated one-band and two-band frames, an exact inverse-Morse factorization, matrix shells and one fixed energy unit through the full depth range. For odd-harmonic analytic potentials, a half-period sign flip gives a lower bound through a closed central gap. In the Aubry--Andr\'e XY chain, for even sources and the specified receivers, the fixed-site light cone follows the typical law at almost every phase; the light cone uniform over sites follows the exact worst-case law when $\beta_e=\beta>L/2$ is finite. For an explicit computable chain these exponents differ and both have Turing degree $\mathbf0''$. We also prove computability of the Lyapunov exponent for computable analytic potentials and a transport exponent of degree $\mathbf0''$ throughout an explicit computable even-potential neighborhood. No computation enters the new upper proof.
\end{abstract}
